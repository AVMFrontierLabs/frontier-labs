Earth observation datasets

The Earth side of every comparison. Entries marked “Used in the lab data” are the sources of the files in the data library. Background entries explain related NASA Earth datasets and are not loaded by the lab.

MODIS Aqua land surface temperature imagery from NASA GIBS and Worldview, part of NASA’s Earth Science Data and Information System (ESDIS).

  2. Preston, L. J., and Dartnell, L. R. (2014). Planetary habitability: lessons learned from terrestrial analogues. International Journal of Astrobiology, 13(1), 81–98. doi:10.1017/S1473550413000396.
